# Pharma 360 Brand Guidelines

Oct 28, 2026 · @Mustafa

Pharma 360 is a pharmacy management platform branded in deep teal and ink with one champagne-gold accent, built around a P-and-medical-cross mark inside an orbit ring. These guidelines record exactly what the brand board and logo file show, and mark anything extra as a recommendation.

## Logo

The mark is one gradient-teal icon made of four parts, always drawn together and never split apart.

1. **P letterform.** A bold, ribbon-style P that folds from dark teal to light teal, standing for Pharma.
2. **Medical cross.** A rounded-corner cross tucked into the bowl of the P, in the same teal gradient, signalling care and pharmacy.
3. **Orbit ring.** Two open arcs, one over the top-left and one under the bottom-right, circling the P and cross. The gaps keep it light and suggest the "360" idea of full coverage.
4. **Gold dot.** A single champagne-gold circle in the upper-right gap of the ring. It is the only warm element in the mark.

The full logo pairs this icon with the wordmark **PHARMA 360** and the descriptor **PHARMACY MANAGEMENT PLATFORM**. The icon-only file you supplied works on both light and dark backgrounds.

## Typography

The board does not name a typeface, so the styles below describe what it shows. The font names are my recommendations and are marked as such.

| Role | Style on the board | Recommended font |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark | Bold geometric sans-serif, all caps. "PHARMA" in Deep Ink, "360" in Primary Teal (white and light teal on dark) | Use the logo file; never retype it |
| Descriptor | "PHARMACY MANAGEMENT PLATFORM" in small all-caps with very wide letter spacing, regular weight, slate grey | Montserrat Medium, tracking about +20% |
| Tagline | Small all-caps, wide tracking, grey, with a short teal rule beneath | Montserrat Medium, tracking about +15% |
| Headings in product | Clean sans-serif, semibold, Deep Ink on light and white on dark | Inter or Montserrat SemiBold |
| Body and tables | Clean sans-serif, regular, high contrast | Inter Regular |
| Key numbers | Large semibold figures, such as Total Sales $24,680, with a small teal trend delta | Inter SemiBold with tabular figures |

Keep headings and wordmark-style text in capitals only where the board does: the wordmark, descriptor and tagline. Product screens use sentence case, as in "Good morning," and the menu labels.

## Tagline and voice

Two lines appear on the board, each in its own place.

| Line | Where it appears | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smarter pharmacy operations. A healthier tomorrow. | Brand statement panel, set in small tracked capitals above a short teal rule | Website hero, pitch decks, brand pages |
| Better Care. Smarter Business. | Back of the business card, beside a vertical divider and a teal rule | Cards, stationery, short promos |

Both lines pair care for patients with smarter operations. Write in the same way: short, calm and confident, with a plain benefit and no hype or medical claims.

## Logo usage rules

The board shows which versions to use but gives no measurements, so the numbers below are recommended starting points for you to confirm.

| Rule | Guidance | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backgrounds | Full-color logo on Light Background or white; reversed logo on Deep Ink or the dark surface; one-color versions on plain grey or near-black | Board |
| Clear space | Keep free space on every side equal to the width of the gold dot | Recommended |
| Minimum size | Icon 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print; horizontal logo 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print | Recommended |
| Descriptor | Drop "PHARMACY MANAGEMENT PLATFORM" below the minimum size and use the icon alone or with the wordmark | Recommended |
| Gold dot | Stays gold in color versions; becomes Deep Ink or white in the one-color versions | Board |
| Gradient | Keep the dark-to-light teal direction as supplied; do not redraw it | Recommended |
| Dark mode | Use the supplied icon unchanged and switch the wordmark to the reversed version | Board |

**Do not**

- Stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the logo, except on dimensional signage
- Separate the cross, P, ring or dot, or move the dot
- Recolor the mark in colors outside the palette
- Place the full-color logo on photos or busy backgrounds
- Set "360" in a color other than Primary Teal or its reversed light teal
